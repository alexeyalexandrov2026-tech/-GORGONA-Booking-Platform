# KA Nails design direction

Status: source reference and design specification; no UI has been built.

The owner-supplied logo is the primary brand asset. Keep its full composition and aspect ratio with generous clear space. Its square ivory canvas, dark espresso KA monogram/wordmark and restrained warm metallic arc point to an editorial luxury direction. Do not retype, recolor, distort or replace it. A transparent derivative or favicon requires a reviewed source-safe export; no derivative was generated here.

Proposed tokens for implementation validation: warm ivory canvas (`#F8F4EE` candidate), espresso text (`#211B18` candidate), warm taupe secondary text (`#756860` candidate), champagne accent (`#B28B77` candidate). Measure actual contrast and sample colors against the original asset before locking tokens. Use an elegant display serif paired with a legible UI sans, spacious layout, restrained borders and subtle motion. Do not reuse Fresh Nails' pink/neon/glow design or its photos as KA Nails work.

Public IA: home, services/details, artists/details, authentic gallery/look details, booking, concierge, appointments, memberships, gift cards, reviews, contact, privacy, terms and policies. Home order: restrained header, strong visual hero, signature services, selected work, artists, concierge, Hammam feature, reviews, loyalty, location/contact and footer. Missing photos and verified business facts must remain unpublished rather than filled with invented content.

Mobile booking: service → artist or any available → soonest slots/calendar → compatible add-ons → customer details → deposit if required → verified confirmation. Keep a live server quote summary and accessible error recovery. Guest booking must work. Validate keyboard, screen reader, WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, visible focus, reduced motion, mobile/tablet/desktop layouts and 200% zoom before launch.
